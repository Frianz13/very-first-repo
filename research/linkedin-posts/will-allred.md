## Post 1 | ~2026-05-15 (≈1 month before collection)
- URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/williamallred_when-was-the-last-time-you-thought-about-activity-7459588294826168320-rGmL
- Tactic : Great cold email copy answers the objections a prospect never says out loud "I'm not the right person," "we tried that," "we already have a vendor," "not a problem for me." Instead of forcing urgency, frame the offer like an insurance policy so it also reaches people who don't yet feel the pain.
- Why I saved it : Anticipating objections before they're spoken hit home, I've felt that unspoken "why would I pay you" resistance myself (got it loud once on Facebook), so this made sense to me.

## Post 2 | ~2026-05-12 (≈1 month before collection)
- URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/williamallred_weve-run-some-cold-email-experiments-for-activity-7458187629520592896-PpGl
- Tactic : Three findings from large-scale cold email experiments: (1) third-party intent data made ~zero difference vs a solid ICP, (2) sending fast after a signal (recency) didn't move reply rates, message quality matters more, except first-party triggers like product usage, (3) deliverability (landing in the inbox) is the real bottleneck: warm domains 3+ months, rotate secondary domains, avoid bulk sends from one inbox.
- Why I saved it : It's all data, not opinion, and it flips the "fancy paid tools = results" myth. That matches how I work: free tools, focus on the fundamentals.

## Post 3 | ~2026-04-15 (≈2 months before collection)
- URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/williamallred_one-of-the-biggest-problems-with-your-cold-activity-7442903238540013568-A5Ux
- Tactic : Stop sending identical messaging to everyone in a company. Same product, different pitch per rol, a CFO cares about hitting the 2-year plan, an FP&A director about forecast volatility, a finance ops analyst about killing manual work. Differentiated messaging requires real buyer knowledge, not generic LLM output.
- Why I saved it: Same offer framed differently per person. For example, it's like how I'd pitch a banner design differently to a small shop vs a bigger shop.
