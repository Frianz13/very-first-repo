## Post 1 | ~2026-05-15 (about 1 month before collection)
- URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/florintatulea_sales-outbound-activity-7453485429091246080-3YxK
- Tactic : Six "signal-based" outbound plays that he claims convert at 3-4x normal rates, each with the data you need to run it: Boomerang (former employees now at a target account), Tech Stack Transition (companies mid-tool-change), Competitive Migration (unhappy customers shopping around), Org Chart Stress (accounts with leadership disruption), Ghost Account Reactivation (re-engaging old interested leads), and Conference + Intent + Title signals.
- Why I saved it : Concrete plays, not vague advice, and finding non-obvious angles like targeting companies mid-change is exactly the kind of clever-angle thinking I enjoy.

## Post 2 | ~2026-01-15 (about 5 months before collection)
- URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/florintatulea_heres-a-stupid-simple-4-step-framework-to-activity-7407121355948593152-eX8c
- Tactic : A 4-step framework for handling cold-call objections, borrowed partly from negotiator Chris Voss: (1) Acknowledge, (2) Label the objection, (3) "That's actually why I called...", (4) ask an open-ended question. He reports it lifted their conversation-to-meeting rate from 7% to 15%, with scripts for budget / wrong-person / "just email me" objections.
- Why I saved it : A repeatable way to handle pushback, with real numbers behind it (7% to 15%). I like that it's a system, and staying calm under objections is something I've had to learn.

## Post 3 | ~2025-12-15 (about 6 months before collection)
- URL : https://www.linkedin.com/posts/florintatulea_the-data-speaks-for-itself-we-found-images-activity-7399473777866264577-wu3A
- Tactic : A/B test result : cold emails with images performed ~3x better than plain text, and relevant memes/visuals cut through inbox noise. He recommends a custom-domain tracking setup (Maildoso) so images and links don't wreck deliverability, letting you put booking links/video in the first email.
- Why I saved it : As a visual designer, "images beat plain text 3x" made instant sense to me! that's literally my world applied to cold outreach.
