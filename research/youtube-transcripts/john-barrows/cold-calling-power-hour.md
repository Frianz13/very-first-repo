[MUSIC PLAYING] One of the best ways to improve your skills and drive results when on the phone is to schedule a power hour. And the way I look at power hours is you bring three, four people at most, into a room with a speakerphone, and everybody comes in with their lists. And what you do is, you all stand up and make round robin phone calls in front of each other.

And what happens there is everybody learns way faster than you would learn otherwise, and we figure out what works and what doesn't work throughout that session. And so think about it. I get up, I stand up, I'm a manager, maybe one of the senior reps, and I get on the phone with somebody, and a secretary or a gatekeeper completely shreds me, hangs up on me mid-sentence.

The person next to me who's up next, they're obviously not going to do what I did. They're going to try to do something different. And when they try an approach, and say they get through and they have a really good conversation, well, obviously, the person after them is going to leverage what they did and avoid what I did.

And in a very short period of time, you learn more than you'll ever learn in any training you'll ever go to, and you'll have some fun doing it. So if there's ever a time in the middle of the day where you're dragging, and it's in the afternoon, and you're just really not feeling the energy, grab three or four people, grab a small conference room and a speakerphone, and all stand up and make calls and learn from each other. Make it happen.

## Video description (from the author)

Perfect your sales process with the Cold Calling Power Hour. Get three or four other sales reps and take turns making calls. You'll learn way more and way faster than if you do it by yourself.
