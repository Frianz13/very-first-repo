## Video description (from the author)

The number of touches it takes to get in contact with a B2B prospect is debatable, but one thing is for sure: one cold call or email won't work.

Instead of going through the motions and putting everything in the first contact, carve up your value proposition to be distributed across five to eight contacts.

One thing you must avoid is referencing failed contact attempts. Give a piece of value with each contact of your prospects.
