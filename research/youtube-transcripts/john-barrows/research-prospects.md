## Video description (from the author)

Before making any cold call you should always put a little bit of effort into researching your prospects.

You don't need to take 30 minutes before each call, but there is no excuse to go in blind when making calls.

At a minimum you need to have a REAL REASON for your call.

You also need to have their website up so you can quickly reference it and understand what they actually do.

I also always AT LEAST check the company's LinkedIn profile for recent news releases, as well as the individual's LinkedIn profile who I'm about to call.
