## Video description (from the author)

The first few words out of your mouth when making cold calls can make or break our chance for engaging.

Get rid of the weak introductions like "How are you doing?" or "Is this a good time?" 

Or the all-time worst: "I'm sorry to annoy you."

Instead you need to lead with powerful introductions like "Thanks for taking my call." 

Another very effective one is "Do you have a few moments?" which quantifies my request for a short amount of their time.

My favorite one for secretaries and gatekeepers is "Can you help me?"

The best introduction in my opinion is "The reason for my call is xyz."

Whatever you do, don't call just to "touch base" and "check in." When you revert to these weak introductions it means there is no real reason for us to talk to one another.
